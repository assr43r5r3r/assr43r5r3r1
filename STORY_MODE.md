# Tetris Story Mode - Design Document

## Overview

The Story Mode transforms Tetris from a simple puzzle game into an immersive narrative experience. Players will follow a protagonist through a world where the ability to manipulate falling blocks has become a mystical power that shapes reality itself.

## Main Character

### Alex "The Shaper"
- **Age:** 22
- **Background:** A young architect student who discovers they have an innate ability to perceive and manipulate geometric patterns in ways others cannot.
- **Personality:** Curious, determined, and initially reluctant about their powers. Grows into a confident leader as the story progresses.
- **Visual Design:** Modern casual style with hints of geometric patterns on clothing. Eyes glow faintly when using abilities.

## Supporting Characters

### Dr. Elena Vex
- **Role:** Mentor figure
- **Background:** A renowned physicist who has studied the phenomenon of "Pattern Shaping" for decades. Discovered Alex's abilities and recruits them.
- **Personality:** Wise, patient, but harboring secrets about her past involvement with the Tetrominoes.

### Kai
- **Role:** Rival / Friend
- **Background:** Another Shaper who works for a competing organization. Initially antagonistic but eventually becomes an ally.
- **Personality:** Confident, competitive, but ultimately good-hearted.

### The Overseer
- **Role:** Main Antagonist
- **Background:** A mysterious figure who controls the "Void" - the chaotic force that creates the falling blocks. Wants to plunge the world into geometric chaos.
- **Personality:** Cold, calculating, speaks in cryptic patterns.

## Plot Summary

### Act I: Awakening (Chapters 1-5)

**Chapter 1: The Beginning**
Alex is an ordinary architecture student until strange geometric visions start haunting their dreams. One day, during a crisis at a construction site, blocks literally start falling from the sky, and Alex instinctively arranges them to save lives.

**Chapter 2: The Institute**
Dr. Vex finds Alex and brings them to the Pattern Institute - a secret organization dedicated to understanding and utilizing Shaping powers. Alex learns the basics of their abilities.

**Chapter 3: First Challenge**
Alex faces their first real test - stabilizing a collapsing building using Shaping. This introduces time-pressure mechanics.

**Chapter 4: Meeting Kai**
Kai challenges Alex to a Shaping duel. Win or lose, this establishes the rivalry and introduces versus-style gameplay.

**Chapter 5: The Void Emerges**
The Overseer makes their first appearance, sending chaos blocks that Alex must clear under difficult conditions.

### Act II: Training (Chapters 6-15)

The middle act focuses on Alex developing their abilities through increasingly complex challenges:
- Speed challenges
- Precision placement
- Limited piece types
- Garbage line removal
- Special objectives

Each chapter reveals more about the world, the history of Shapers, and the threat of the Void.

### Act III: Confrontation (Chapters 16-25)

The final act leads to the confrontation with the Overseer:
- Alex learns the truth about the Void and its connection to pattern manipulation
- Kai joins as an ally after being betrayed by the Overseer
- Dr. Vex's secrets are revealed
- The final battle requires Alex to demonstrate mastery of all techniques learned

## Chapter Structure

Each chapter consists of:
1. **Cutscene Introduction** - Story context and dialogue
2. **Challenge Briefing** - Gameplay objectives explained
3. **Gameplay Segment** - The actual Tetris challenge with unique rules
4. **Cutscene Conclusion** - Story resolution and setup for next chapter
5. **Score Summary** - Performance rating (1-3 stars)

## Gameplay Modifiers

Story mode introduces unique gameplay elements:

### Special Block Types
- **Void Blocks** - Must be cleared within 3 turns or they spread
- **Anchor Blocks** - Cannot be moved once placed
- **Ghost Blocks** - Phase through other blocks temporarily
- **Charged Blocks** - Explode and clear adjacent blocks when part of a line

### Challenge Types
- **Speed Trial** - Clear lines before time runs out
- **Survival** - Last as long as possible with increasing difficulty
- **Precision** - Must clear specific patterns
- **Protection** - Keep certain areas clear of blocks
- **Versus AI** - Compete against Kai or other Shapers

## Dialogue System

Story Mode features:
- Character portraits with emotional expressions
- Dialogue choices that affect minor story elements
- Optional side conversations with supporting characters
- Unlockable lore entries about the world

## Unlockables

Completing story chapters unlocks:
- New color themes based on story locations
- Character-themed Tetromino skins
- Background music tracks
- Concept art gallery
- Challenge mode variations

## Technical Notes

The story mode is built on top of the existing stage system:
- Each chapter maps to specific stage configurations
- Additional UI layers handle dialogue and cutscenes
- Save data tracks completion status, stars earned, and dialogue choices
